# Changelog

## 0.1.0

First release.

- Space in Nautilus previews the selected file, replacing Sushi without
  plugins or patches. Space again closes the preview.
- Viewers for photos (zoom, EXIF rotation, GIF and WebP animation), video,
  music (cover art, tags, waveform), PDF, Word, Excel, PowerPoint and
  OpenDocument (through LibreOffice, cached), Markdown, code with
  highlighting, CSV and TSV, folders, archives and fonts.
- The first frame shows in about 10 to 20 ms. Neighbouring photos decode
  ahead of time, so the arrow keys step through a folder instantly.
- Follows the Omarchy theme accent, window rounding and language (English,
  Slovak, Czech, German).
- `gander FILE` previews from a terminal or a keybinding.
